# HealthLogue

**Your health, remembered. Your days, on track.**

HealthLogue is an AI health companion that keeps a complete memory of your health and checks in with you every day, so staying healthy doesn't depend on remembering to open an app.

## The problem

Most people already track their health. A watch or ring counts sleep and steps, an app logs meals, a pharmacy app holds prescriptions, and doctor visits live in a folder of PDFs. The data is everywhere, but it rarely changes what anyone does tomorrow. People know what they should do. Day to day, nobody helps them actually do it.

## What HealthLogue does

**A 360° health memory.** Medications, vitals, sleep, activity, meals, lab reports, doctor visits and voice notes, all in one private record that builds up over time.

**A daily check-in that feels like a person.** Ava, HealthLogue's voice assistant, calls you for a short check-in. She already knows your day, so she only asks what matters, like a dose you missed, a change your doctor made, or a promise you made yesterday. On a good day, the call is short.

**Advice turned into action.** HealthLogue turns what it learns into one or two concrete things to do today, then follows up on them.

**Automatic by default.** You shouldn't have to ask questions or build routines. HealthLogue notices, suggests and follows up on its own.

**Built with the people who help you.** Family members and caregivers can stay in the loop, with each person choosing what they share.

## Principles

- **Your data is yours.** Private by default, shared only with people you choose, deletable at any time.
- **Supportive, not clinical.** HealthLogue helps you follow your care plan. It does not diagnose, and it never changes a medication. Medical decisions stay with you and your doctor.
- **Built for the world.** Multilingual voice from day one, for people and families everywhere.
